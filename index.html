Exploring Multimodal AI, NLP, Computer Vision, and LLM for Real-World Problems.

I am Labanya Saha, a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from the University of Asia Pacific, Bangladesh. My research interests lie at the intersection of Large language models,machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and multimodal learning, with particular interest in low-resource and data-constrained settings.

I am interested in developing intelligent and reliable AI systems that can connect research with practical problems. I am currently strengthening my research experience and preparing for PhD applications for Fall 2028.

Ongoing · Audio & Multimodal AI

Audio Deepfake Detection: A Systematic Review

Conducting a systematic review of research on audio deepfake and synthetic speech detection. Reviewing existing deep learning, speech representation, and large language model-based approaches, while analyzing datasets, evaluation methods, and research trends to identify challenges and potential directions for future research.

Ongoing · Medical AI & LLMs

LLM-Assisted Medical Research

Exploring the use of large language models and agent-based approaches for medical research, including working with biomedical literature and structured datasets to support evidence extraction, information organization, and research-oriented reasoning. The work also involves analyzing and integrating information from research papers and domain-specific datasets to support systematic investigation.

Ongoing · Computer Vision

Computer Vision for Automated Mulberry Leaf Disease Classification

Investigating deep learning approaches for mulberry leaf disease classification using image data. Studying model performance under variations in image quality and visual conditions. Exploring controlled image perturbations including illumination changes, blur, noise, and resolution degradation. Comparing baseline and robustness-oriented training approaches using transfer learning. Implementing experiments using Python and PyTorch.
